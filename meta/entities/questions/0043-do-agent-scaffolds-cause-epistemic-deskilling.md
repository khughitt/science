# Do agent scaffolds cause epistemic deskilling?

## Summary

Does sustained use of LLM-agent workflows for hypothesis formulation, literature synthesis, causal-DAG construction, and evidence interpretation erode the researcher's independent critical judgment — leaving them progressively less able to detect errors in generated structure, validate causal claims independently, or recognize a confabulated literature anchor — such that the toolkit increases epistemic error rates after an initial productivity gain?

**Exploration rationale (one line per contributing lens):**

- _contrarian_: Attacks the premise that scaffolding improves the researcher, arguing the mechanism of improvement is precisely what scaffolding removes.

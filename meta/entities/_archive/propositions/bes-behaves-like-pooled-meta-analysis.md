# BES behaves like pooled meta-analysis

Bayesian Evidence Synthesis behaves like pooled meta-analysis when aggregating
study evidence.

This proposition is intentionally broad enough to be contested by the existing
BES summaries: the literature notes similarity in some settings, while also
warning that BES is not data pooling and can diverge when studies are weak or
boundary-adjacent.

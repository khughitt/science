# Proposition: Patchwork representation is triangulated as faithful across metaphysics, cosmology, and philosophy of science

## Claim

The Science working model's patchwork/federated representation — locally-valid,
purpose-indexed, uncertainty-bearing models rather than a pyramid deducible from
universal laws — is a *faithful* representation of scientific knowledge, not a
deficiency of the graph. The claim is triangulated from three disjoint directions:
metaphysics (Mumford's realist lawlessness — properties are power-bearing, so
distinct governing laws are explanatorily redundant), cosmology (Gaspar and Tambor
— cosmological evidence forbids a static universal law-set, and the Standard
Cosmological Model itself behaves as a nomological machine), and philosophy of
science (Frigg/Hartmann's autonomous mediating models and Giere's purpose-indexed
representation). Each patch is a Cartwright nomological machine: a law/relation that
holds only within its enabling conditions.

## Evidence Summary

*Evidence type: literature_evidence (convergent, cross-disciplinary).*
Three independent literatures converge on the same anti-Humean, patchwork
conclusion. Mumford grounds it metaphysically ("the world is more of a jigsaw than
a mosaic") [@Mumford2004]; Gaspar and Tambor show the same patchwork is forced by
physics, naming Smolin–Unger's *false universality* and *false anachronism* as
methodological errors [@Gaspar2024]; Frigg/Hartmann report the Cartwright/Hacking
patchwork-of-models thesis as the mainstream philosophy-of-science position, and
Giere supplies the operational four-place schema (*S uses X to represent W for
purposes P*) under which multiple purpose-relative models of one target coexist
without conflict [@Frigg2025; @Giere2004]. The convergence across disjoint routes,
rather than reliance on a single source, is what makes it load-bearing for
`hypothesis:0007-working-model`.

## Caveats

The philosophy core is pre-computational: none of these sources advises on
serialization or graph schemas, so the mapping from "patchwork is faithful" to the
toolkit's patch entity is an architectural conjecture, not a settled requirement.
[@Mumford2004] is a Chapter-1 publisher preview, so its positive powers account is
accessed only through its own summary. The named common ancestor of the
convergence — Cartwright (1999), *The Dappled World* — is unread (see task t094), so
every downstream claim about the dappled world is currently second-hand. The
faithfulness claim is also conditional on the pragmatic/agent-based reading of
representation; a strict isomorphism/correspondence reading (Ghins) would frame the
same patchwork as a limitation to be reduced rather than a faithful representation
(the open axis in question:0029).

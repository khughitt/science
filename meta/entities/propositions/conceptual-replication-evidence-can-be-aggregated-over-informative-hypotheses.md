# Conceptual-replication evidence can be aggregated over informative hypotheses

Bayesian Evidence Synthesis and product Bayes factor methods can aggregate study-level
support for a shared informative hypothesis across heterogeneous conceptual-replication
studies without treating the result as an ordinary pooled effect-size estimate.

# Evidence Line: Models-as-mediators and the patchwork-of-models thesis

## What this line shows

Frigg and Hartmann's SEP survey reports the Cartwright/Hacking patchwork-of-models
thesis as the mainstream philosophy-of-science position and legitimizes models as
autonomous mediators that represent through partial, purpose-selected, ineliminably
idealized resemblance [@Frigg2025]. This supports the proposition from the
philosophy-of-science-of-models direction.

## Why it is independent

A survey of the models literature, independent of the metaphysics-of-powers
(Mumford) and cosmology (Gaspar) routes; it aggregates a distinct body of work
(model representation, idealization, Levins's trade-off).

## Caveats / scope

Moderate strength: a survey rather than a primary argument, and it itself defers to
Cartwright (1999) as the load-bearing source (still unread; task t094). Frigg's own
perspectival realism sits between the pragmatic and correspondence readings, so the
"faithful" framing is conditional on the pragmatic reading (question:0029).

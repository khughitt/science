# Evidence Line: Mumford's realist lawlessness grounds the patchwork metaphysically

## What this line shows

Mumford argues that properties are intrinsically modal (power-bearing), so distinct
"governing laws" are explanatorily redundant — "the world is more of a jigsaw than a
mosaic" [@Mumford2004]. This is the metaphysical route to the claim that a
locally-valid, patch-structured representation is faithful to how the world is
organized, not a shortfall of the graph.

## Why it is independent

This is a metaphysics-of-laws argument (powers/dispositional realism) developed
independently of the cosmological (Gaspar) and philosophy-of-science-of-models
(Frigg/Giere) routes that support the same proposition; it shares no data source,
method, or literature with them.

## Caveats / scope

Moderate strength: the source is a Chapter-1 publisher preview, so the positive
powers account and objection-and-reply material are inaccessible, and the claim is
read from Mumford's own summary. The argument is pre-computational and does not by
itself establish the toolkit-schema mapping.

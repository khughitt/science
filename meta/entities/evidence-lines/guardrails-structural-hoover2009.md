# Evidence Line: Hoover's structural identity conditions ground the same-system check

## What this line shows

Hoover grounds causal order in a privileged (variation-free) parameterization: two
causal systems are identical iff they share variables, parameter space, and
functional form, and modularity can hold at the parameter level yet fail at the
mechanism level (carburetors, the Lucas critique) [@Hoover2009]. This supports the
proposition's structural-identity half: "same estimand" is insufficient for "same
system", so a guardrail must check structural identity before strengthening an edge.

## Why it is independent

An econometrics/philosophy-of-causality argument about the identity conditions of
causal systems, independent of the proxy-identifiability (DeCaFlow) line that
supports the same proposition from the estimand-recovery direction.

## Caveats / scope

Moderate strength: structural identity requires knowing the parameterization, often
unavailable for literature-derived evidence, so the check may be inapplicable where
most needed and must degrade to an explicit "structure unknown" state. The source is
pre-computational relative to the toolkit's payload schema.

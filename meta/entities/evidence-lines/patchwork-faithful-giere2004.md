# Evidence Line: Giere's purpose-indexed representation licenses coexisting local models

## What this line shows

Giere's four-place schema — *S uses X to represent W for purposes P* — makes
representation an agent-and-purpose-indexed act with no need for an objective
similarity measure, so multiple purpose-relative models of one target coexist
without conflict (water-as-molecules vs. water-as-fluid) [@Giere2004]. This directly
supports the claim that a patchwork of locally-valid, purpose-indexed models is the
faithful representation, and supplies the missing `purpose` slot for the patch.

## Why it is independent

A pragmatic/agent-based account of scientific representation, developed
independently of the powers metaphysics (Mumford) and cosmology (Gaspar) routes;
distinct argument and literature.

## Caveats / scope

Moderate strength: Giere's pragmatism is one pole of an unresolved axis against
Ghins's judgement/correspondence account, so it grounds the "faithful patchwork"
claim only under the pragmatic reading the toolkit has not yet explicitly adopted
(question:0029). Pre-computational with respect to the schema.

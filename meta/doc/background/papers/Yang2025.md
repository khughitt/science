# Causal Mediation Analysis for Integrating Exposure, Genomic, and Phenotype Data

- **Authors:** Haoyu Yang et al.
- **Year:** 2025
- **Journal:** Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-statistics-040622-031653
- **BibTeX key:** Yang2025
- **Source:** PDF

## Key Contribution

Yang et al. review causal mediation analysis as a framework for integrating exposure, genomic, omics, and phenotype data [@Yang2025].
The paper is important for Science because mediation provides a causal graph pattern for mechanism claims: exposure affects outcome through measured intermediates.

## Methods

The review covers single-mediator and high-dimensional mediation analysis, including multiple mediators, multiple exposures, composite null hypotheses, false discovery rate control, simulations, and a Normative Aging Study example.
It focuses on statistical inference for mediation effects, especially in high-dimensional genomic settings.

## Key Findings

The paper emphasizes that testing mediation effects is a composite-null problem [@Yang2025].
High-dimensional mediation requires careful multiple-testing control and attention to assumptions.
Identification of natural indirect effects depends on no unmeasured exposure-outcome, exposure-mediator, and mediator-outcome confounding, plus no exposure-induced mediator-outcome confounding and no exposure-mediator interaction under the simple reviewed model.

## Relevance

Science should not let "mechanism" edges bypass causal assumptions.
Mediation claims need exposure, mediator, outcome, confounder set, natural direct/indirect effect target, identification assumptions, multiple-testing correction, and sensitivity diagnostics.
This directly extends H04 from total effects to mechanistic causal pathways.

## Limitations

The review emphasizes assumptions that are often difficult to verify.
Mediation analysis can be fragile under unmeasured confounding, exposure-induced confounding, and interaction.
High-dimensional mediation increases multiplicity and selection concerns.

## Model / Tool Availability

The review compares methods but does not center on a single new software package.

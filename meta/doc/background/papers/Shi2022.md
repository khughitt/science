# Data Integration in Causal Inference

- **Authors:** Xu Shi, Ziyang Pan, and Wang Miao
- **Year:** 2022
- **Journal:** WIREs Computational Statistics
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/wics.1581
- **BibTeX key:** Shi2022
- **Source:** PDF

## Key Contribution

Shi et al. review causal inference methods that integrate heterogeneous datasets collected under different designs, populations, or variable sets [@Shi2022].
The central contribution for Science is a compact taxonomy of causal data integration: combining trials with observational data, joining incomplete variable samples, distributed data, Bayesian causal inference, and causal discovery.

## Methods

The paper reviews potential-outcome notation, consistency, treatment exchangeability, treatment positivity, selection exchangeability, and selection positivity.
It then surveys methods for combining randomized clinical trials with external observational or historical data, combining datasets when no sample has all variables, distributed privacy-preserving causal inference, Bayesian causal inference, and causal discovery.

## Key Findings

The review emphasizes that average treatment effects are target-population-specific [@Shi2022].
Data integration can improve efficiency, generalizability, or bias correction, but only under explicit assumptions about confounding, selection, positivity, transportability, and missing variable structure.
When no single dataset has all relevant variables, identification becomes difficult even for otherwise standard estimands.

## Relevance

This paper directly strengthens H04 and Q02.
Science should represent trial/observational design, sample-selection mechanism, target population, treatment and selection exchangeability assumptions, positivity, privacy/distributed constraints, and variable-overlap structure before integrated evidence updates causal claims.

## Limitations

The paper is a broad review and does not prescribe one representation schema.
It focuses on treatment-effect inference rather than all forms of causal graph construction.
It gives conceptual coverage rather than a project-ready validator.

## Model / Tool Availability

No single software package is released with the review.
